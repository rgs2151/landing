## Nature

We introduce a mouse paradigm to study cooperative behavior where stable leader-follower roles emerge during joint foraging. Using calcium imaging and optogenetic disruption, the study shows medial prefrontal cortex representations are role-specific and critical for cooperation. I developed the forward-modeling framework paired with multi-agent inverse reinforcement learning to decode latent value functions driving cooperative decisions.

## LLM Steering

We formulate LLM steering as a feedback-control problem inspired by closed-loop neurostimulation. Our robust controller adapts interventions to the model's evolving internal state while accounting for uncertainty in its dynamics, improving steering under shifts in language, adversarial prompts, and context length without changing model weights. We also introduce robust steerability, a pre-deployment measure of how much dynamics mismatch an intervention can tolerate, connecting reliable control in artificial and biological neural systems.

## LFP

We develop a framework for recovering the geometry of local field potentials, revealing the low-dimensional structure behind population-level brain signals. Under an autoregressive model, we show how sustained oscillatory modes give rise to toroidal manifolds through lag embedding, while noise and aperiodic activity contribute geometric thickness. A geometry-aware learning network extends reconstruction to time-varying dynamics, with validation on synthetic systems and neural recordings across species.
