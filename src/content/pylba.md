PyLBA is a Python package for fitting and simulating Linear Ballistic Accumulator (LBA) models commonly used in cognitive psychology and neuroscience. The LBA provides a mathematically tractable account of decision-making, jointly explaining choice probabilities and response-time distributions through independent evidence accumulators. Brown & Heathcote, 2008. PyLBA provides a unified interface for model specification, estimation, and simulation. Flexible Bayesian fitting supports custom priors and fixed or freely estimated parameters. Structured parameter management accommodates shared and accumulator-specific configurations. Synthetic data generation connects fitted models to their behavioral predictions. Its extensible architecture allows additional accumulator models to use the same interface.
