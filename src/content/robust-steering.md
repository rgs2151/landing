In this co-first-author work, we introduce robust closed-loop steering of large language models, bringing principles of neurostimulation to AI control. Our feedback controller adjusts activation interventions to the model's evolving internal state and explicitly accounts for uncertainty, outperforming existing steering methods under distribution shifts without retraining the model. Beyond steering performance, we introduce robust steerability: a measure of how much uncertainty an intervention can withstand, turning calibration-time dynamics into a diagnostic of reliable control before deployment.
