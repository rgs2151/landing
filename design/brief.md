# Design Brief

The redesign will be maximal, highly interactive, and heavily animated. It may
include multiple videos playing alongside other animations. Choose libraries
to support the agreed interactions as prototypes develop.

Use shared content, media, and reusable behavior across prototype variations.
Do not prescribe page sections or a component hierarchy before reviewing the
user's references. Content changes require an explicit request.

Iterate through local browser prototypes before implementing the selected
direction in the main website.

## Agreed Entrance

The entrance expresses the user's taste to a general audience. Readable project
views carry achievements for researchers, collaborators, and hiring teams.

Use dense layering, large lettering, rough texture, and collage made from the
user's work. Colors come from meaningful project figures and media. Avoid old
computer-window styling. Use a consistent particle language inspired by
https://github.com/Jakubantalik/thinking-orbs across biological and artificial
representations. A hidden brain structure remains an exploratory possibility.

Keep the face and contact details in a corner. About content is revealed
separately. Hover fans out the central composition; click holds the selection
and opens a calmer, readable project view. Mobile gets a simple photo,
introduction, contact details, and an invitation to open the desktop experience.

Prototype A uses still frames, a static particle sketch, and simple hover
transitions to assess composition and interaction before detailed animation.
The LLM steering sketch is an exploration, not an asserted project achievement.
